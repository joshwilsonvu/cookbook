---
course: side
author: Gabby
ingredients:
  - 1 can diced tomatoes, about 15 oz
  - about 1/2 jalepeño
  - 1/2 tsp adobo
  - 2 tsp lemon juice
  - 2 tsp lime juice
  - 1-2 cloves garlic
  - 1 bunch (handful) cilantro
---

1. Add all ingredients to a food processor and purée until smooth.
2. Chill in a bowl and serve.
