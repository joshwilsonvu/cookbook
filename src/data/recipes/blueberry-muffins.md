---
course: dessert
author: Gabby
ingredients:
  - 1 cup milk
  - 1 egg
  - 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  - 2 cups all-purpose flour
  - 2 tsp baking powder
  - 1/2 cup sugar
  - 1 cup blueberries
---

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Mix ingredients except blueberries in a large mixing bowl. Then, gently fold the blueberries into the mixture.
3. Line a muffin pan with wrappers and pour batter into each one until they are at least 3/4 full.
4. Bake for about 20 minutes.
5. Insert a toothpick to check for doneness; it's done if it comes out clean.
