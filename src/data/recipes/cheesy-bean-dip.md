---
title: Cheesy Bean Dip
course: dessert
author: Josh
ingredients:
  - 1 (16‑oz) can refried beans
  - 2/3 jar (≈10 oz) smooth red salsa
  - 5 oz (½ cup) cream cheese, softened
  - 8 oz shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  - ½ tsp ground cumin
  - ½ tsp chili powder
  - 'Optional: chopped cilantro for garnish'

---

1. Place the refried beans, salsa, cream cheese, shredded cheddar, cumin, and chili powder in the slow‑cooker. 2. Stir until the mixture is mostly uniform; the cream cheese will melt as it heats. 3. Cover and cook on HIGH for 20–30 minutes, stirring once or twice to help the flavors meld. 4. Keep the dip on “warm” until ready to serve. 5. Serve directly from the slow‑cooker with tortilla chips and, if desired, a sprinkle of cilantro.
