---
course: side
author: Gabby
ingredients:
  - 6–8 potatoes
  - 2 tsp salt
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
---

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Peel all potatoes.
3. Chop potatoes into different shapes.
4. Place potatoes on a baking sheet, sprinkle with salt, and drizzle with olive oil. Toss together.
5. Put into oven for 20-23 minutes.
6. Enjoy!
