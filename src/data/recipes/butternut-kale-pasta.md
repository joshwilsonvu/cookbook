---
title: Butternut Kale Pasta
course: dessert
author: Josh
ingredients:
  - 8 oz rigatoni or fusilli pasta
  - 1 small delicata squash (≈12 oz peeled, cubed butternut squash)
  - 1 bunch kale, stems removed and leaves chopped
  - 2 Tbsp olive oil, plus a little extra for the squash
  - 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  - ½ lemon, zest and juice
  - ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese, plus extra for serving
  - Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - Walnuts, chopped (optional, ≈¼ cup)

---

1. Preheat oven to 425 °F. Halve, seed, and cut the delicata squash into half‑moons. Toss with a drizzle of olive oil, salt, and pepper; spread on a sheet pan and roast 20–25 min, turning once, until tender and browned. 2. Meanwhile, cook pasta in salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain. 3. In a large skillet, heat 2 Tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add sliced garlic (and walnuts, if using) and sauté ~30 s until fragrant. Add chopped kale and a splash of reserved pasta water; cook, stirring, until kale wilts, about 5 min. 4. Add cooked pasta, roasted squash, lemon zest, lemon juice, and Parmesan. Stir, adding pasta water a splash at a time, until the sauce coats the pasta glossy. Adjust seasoning with salt and black pepper. 5. Plate and finish with additional Parmesan.
